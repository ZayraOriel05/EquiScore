## EquiScore - FinTech Project for AI-Powered Credit Scoring Model

Themes: Credit Score, Microfinance, Train AI 

A credit score application that helps people with microfinancing pay back their loans, earn their loans back, not be in debt, 


TARGET AUDIENCES:
Women, 68 - 90 percent target group, examples of this are

Our app targets individuals with low credit scores who need access to microloans. It helps users build healthier financial habits, set and achieve repayment goals, improve their creditworthiness over time, and develop greater financial literacy to make informed financial decisions.
